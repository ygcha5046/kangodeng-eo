📖 Fish Encyclopedia

🐟

Normal Mackerel

The standard blue-backed mackerel commonly found on dining tables. Rich in DHA and loved globally as a nutritious staple.

🥉

Bronze Mackerel

A rare variant with a heavy bronze tint. Known for its firmer texture and deeper, savory flavor compared to regular breeds.

🥈

Silver Mackerel

Radiant like moonlight, this silver-coated fish is legendary among anglers, celebrated for its exceptionally soft texture.

👑

Golden Mackerel

The hidden treasure of the sea! Covered in gold from head to tail, landing one is said to bring eternal fortune.

🍳 Signature Recipes

Grilled Mackerel

Crispy on the outside, moist on the inside. Perfectly salted and inherently oily, making it the ultimate companion for a bowl of rice.

Braised Mackerel

Simmered slowly with thick slices of radish and aged kimchi in a spicy-sweet sauce. A flavor-packed traditional favorite.

Mackerel Bites

Boneless mackerel chunks fried to a crisp and tossed in a sweet and spicy glaze. A delightful treat loved by all ages.
